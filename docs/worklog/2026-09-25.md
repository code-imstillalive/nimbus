### The move that cracked it: an instrument from a different pipeline

The `feedback_nimbus_stop_daily_release_churn` lesson — *check the record first, cross-check against an independent instrument* — is what worked, and it worked in one step. `sensor.lv_v2_p2p_confirmed_history` carries the real settled LocalVolts revenue, produced by a pipeline with no connection to the scorer:

| day | settled P2P export | volume | EPR published |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 20 Sep | **$4.01** | 21.9 kWh | **88.6%** |
| 21 Sep | $14.91 | 49.4 kWh | 51.9% |
| 22 Sep | $12.71 | 57.9 kWh | 38.5% |
| 23 Sep | $13.46 | 50.0 kWh | 36.4% |

**Anti-correlated with the money.** The scorer read worst on the household's best-earning days and read 88.6% on the one day it genuinely under-exported by half. That is not a calibration problem, it is a sign error in what the metric is tracking — and it settled "is this the score or the dispatch?" before a line of the scorer was opened.

It also has a consequence worth stating plainly: **EPR on this install could not have detected a real dispatch failure.** #1179 records 59% of solve cycles failing for 2.7 h inside the P2P window on the night of 20 Sep — the day that scored best.

### Root cause

`quality_report.py:749` — `j_ach = j_ach_residual.total_cost - real_p2p_dollars_earned`.

A day is first scored just after local midnight. Its settlement does not arrive until **~09:00 local the following morning** (confirmed from the settlement sensor's own `last_updated`: 2026-09-23T23:00Z = 24 Sep 09:00 AEST, carrying `latest_date: 2026-09-23`). Until it does, `real_p2p_dollars` is 0 — and that *also* makes the `real_p2p_volume_kwh > 0.01` bonus gate false, so the bonus-priced `grid_oracle` rebuild never runs either. `j_ref`, `j_ach` and `j_star` go P2P-blind **together**, and the day is priced as though the household had no P2P arrangement at all.

#1082 already existed for exactly this, and was genuinely on production (shipped v0.94.380, well before 0.94.413). Its repair is reachable only from inside the `latest_date == yesterday_key` fast path, **so it expires at local midnight.** The production log:

```
2026-09-24 01:00  2026-09-23 was scored before its settlement was available
2026-09-24 02:00-06:00  same, hourly
2026-09-24 06:00  LAST firing
2026-09-24 09:00  settlement for 23 Sep arrives ($13.46)   <- nothing rescores it
2026-09-25 00:00  yesterday_key rolls over; the row is frozen at 36.4%
```

Identical shape on 22 Sep. **Why it stopped at 06:00 is not established.** The 06:00 ML retrain does starve the solve cycle (`previous cycle still in progress -- skipping this tick`, up to 5 consecutive skips) but that burst ends at 06:07, which does not explain silence until midnight. Left on #1200 as an open question rather than given a tidy cause, because this issue's history is tidy causes that did not survive.

### What shipped — v0.94.416

- **`"p"` on a history row** whose figures were computed without settlement. Written only when provisional, self-clearing on re-score, one character. The attribute payload was already measured 27% over #944's 16 KB cap, so correct rows must cost nothing — hence the opposite convention to `"r"`, which is always written. Safe here because the sweep reads an absent key as "nothing to do": a wrongly-absent key costs a missed repair, never a wrong one.
- **`repair_provisional_quality_history()`** — re-scores any *past* flagged row once the real settlement entry is present. **No deadline.** Gated on `_settlement_entry_exists()` so a day whose settlement never arrives costs **zero** solves rather than one MILP per cycle, which would be #773/#757's executor starvation rebuilt on purpose. One day per cycle, oldest first. Checks *presence*, not a non-zero figure, because a settled day of genuinely zero export is a real result.
- **A conditional "Re-score with settlement" button on the Regret card.** Household ask: *"can we actually just add this rescore fix script in a form of a clickable button on the forecaster view next to epr table?"* Built into the card rather than as a dashboard button — it then needs **no lovelace edit at all**, which matters because that dashboard is under manual redesign and carries card-mod highlights that have been lost five times. Shown only when the displayed day is actually waiting; an always-present button invites buying oracle solves on a day that is already correct.

It also explains something that would otherwise read as a failed rescore: `generated_at` still says `06:00` on a report whose figures were recomputed in the afternoon. That is **#1220 sighted live on production**, and its fix is in the undeployed .419.

### #1228 — why `epr_reliable` never flipped

The household's notes predicted that deploying Mark's #949 (fleet-blended SoC comparison) would clear the long-standing `epr_reliable: False`, and instructed reopening #949 if it did not. It deployed; it did not clear; and reopening would have been wrong.

**#949 is a structural no-op on this install.** `scored_participants: ['home']`, and `fleet_achieved_energy_in_kwh == achieved_energy_in_kwh == 107.965` to three decimals. One battery participant means a capacity-weighted blend has nothing to blend. The fix is right for multi-participant installs and was never what produced this flag.

The real cause is a shape mismatch: `ach.soc_pct` is an hourly **mean** (`_hourly_means_by_key`), `real_pct` is a **point sample at `HH:00:00`** (`resample_history_nearest`). Differencing a mean against an instant injects about half the hour's ramp rate as artifact. The published `max 21.17` lands on hour 12 — the steepest charge ramp of the day, +26.90 pt/hr, predicted artifact 13.45 pt. Like for like the day reads max 11.82 / mean 6.46 and passes both thresholds (15.0 / 8.0).

**Two hypotheses died first, both by recomputation rather than argument**, and the second is the one worth recording:

- *Boundary exemption not firing at the 2.0% floor.* Real SoC sits exactly on the envelope floor at hours 05-06 with `boundary_exempted: False`. Reading `_soc_discrepancy` showed #571's exemption only fires when the *achieved* trajectory leaves [0, 100], and achieved sat at 8.88%. Correct as designed.
- *Efficiency applied twice.* The charge and discharge legs sit at 0.848 and 1.152; `eta**2` at the configured 0.9263 is 0.858 and 1.166 — a match within ~1% **on two legs of opposite sign**, which is not a coincidence you expect from noise. It was still wrong. Recomputing the whole trajectory with the eta term removed moved the mean gap 9.88 → 9.07. A five-line script was the only thing between that and a confidently wrong issue.

**The fix was deliberately not implemented.** The obvious one-liner collides with `resample_history_mean()`'s own docstring — *"SoC and price are real STATE values (sample-and-hold is the physically correct model for those, not an average)"* — which is a considered prior decision and correct as stated. So there are two coherent fixes, the codebase's own principle points at the less obvious one, and it is @purcell-lab's call. The "Proposed fix" section I had already published was corrected in a follow-up comment rather than left standing.

Two residuals survive the correction and are genuinely physical, so the flag should keep existing even while firing for the wrong reason: a BMS recalibration to the 2.0% floor at 04:00-05:00 (real fell 9.70 pt on 4.95 kWh measured), and the power sensor under-reading 12-21% at 19-32 kW charge.

### #1179 — instrumented (PR #1229)

The issue named what would settle it and nobody had done it: whether the *failing* solves are the ones that took the minimum-weight blend fallback. On 20 Sep, 44 blend warnings against 153 failures, **37 sharing only a timestamp-second** — correlated by clock, not by cycle.
