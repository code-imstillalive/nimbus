## The P2P block delivered 10.6 kW instead of 12 (#1610, v0.94.442)

**Seen by the household at 21:50.** In the 17:00-24:00 block the battery ran
12.0 kW, the house drew ~1.1 kW and the meter read -10.6 kW. Their rule: the block
rate is net to the grid, with the house covered on top.

**Measured.**
- The setpoint stepped 13.1 -> 12.0 at 21:11:37, the minute the buy price fell
  0.207 -> 0.184 $/kWh.
- The plan for that period showed **grid export 12.0 and grid import 1.11 together**;
  its own flow breakdown was battery->grid 10.89, battery->load 1.10.
- On 1-4 Oct the buy price went under 20c in 26-35 block slots per evening and the
  setpoint never fell to the block rate. On 6-7 Oct it fell every time.

**Root cause, by replay.** `solver_writer.main()` was run standalone against
production's REST API, with every non-GET request refused and state writes captured
in memory. Same data, within a minute:

| replay | battery now | plan grid import |
|---|---|---|
| v0.94.439, production's sensors | 12.00 | 1.08 |
| v0.94.439, the pre-6-Oct LocalVolts sensors | 11.99 | 1.08 |
| v0.94.435 (ran 1-4 Oct) | 11.99 | 1.10 |
| v0.94.439 + no grid import in pinned periods | 13.21 | 0.0 |

So it was **not** 439 and **not** the LocalVolts v2 sensor switch. `fixed_export_kw`
pins gross `grid_export[t]` and left `grid_import[t]` free. Whenever buying was below
the LP's value of stored energy, it bought the house load and still counted the full
block. 1-4 Oct's inputs never made that worth it; 6-7 Oct's did.

Two other leads were tested and dropped before acting on them:
- The v2 array stamps rows at interval start, not end.
- Under 0.94.438, `resample_real_p2p_rate` read zero for `intervalEnd`-keyed rows.
  That was real, and fixed in 439 by #1537, but irrelevant to this: the pin binds
  whether or not the premium is on.

**Fix, #1610.**
- `p2p_export.grid_import_ub()` is 0 in any pinned period, from any block at any rate.
- #390's penalised `grid_import_excess` keeps the plan feasible when the battery
  covers the block but not the house.
- A battery that cannot cover the block itself was already infeasible and still is.
- The period-0 price-spike override keeps its own limit.
- 7 tests; 3 fail on `main` without it.

**Release, v0.94.442.**
- Built from `release/0.94.442`: v0.94.441 plus #1610 only, by household choice.
- This is the LP-behaviour category `docs/release-process.md` holds overnight. The
  household decided same-night tagging was fine and that they deploy after midnight.
  That decision is in the release notes.
- devhub at ec2a52f: restart clean, solve `optimal`, no new errors. The P2P behaviour
  itself can't be observed there (#1396 mirror). It was verified by replaying
  production's live solve with the release tree: block at 13 gave battery 14.5,
  import 0, net 13.
- Tag on `c7f9c05c`, published 22:33. #1611 records the section on `main`.
- **Not deployed yet.** The household raised block 1 to 13 kW at 22:08 as a
  workaround; it must go back to 12 at deploy.
