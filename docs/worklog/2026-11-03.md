## Battery Sell Margin (#2228, #2229; 0.94.506)

Overnight on the reference household the plan held the battery at about 18 % and bought from the grid at 18-20 c. Cause: Discharge Cost, raised to 5 c on 14 Oct to stop the plan selling against a low near-term forecast (#1749), prices every kWh out of the battery, house cover included.
