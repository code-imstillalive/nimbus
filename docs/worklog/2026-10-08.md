## The laundry phantom load (#1632, a duplicate of #1556)

The household asked about phantom load. The laundry circuit's forecast held
0.36-0.44 kW around the clock against a normal-week average of 0.12 kW
(27 full-household days; 18 Sep - 5 Oct was a holiday with one person home).
Replaying `train_model` on 44 days of real history:

| training data | points kept (of 4,224) | mean |
|---|---:|---:|
| idle stretches held | 4,224 | 0.077 kW |
| 45-minute staleness cap (pre-#1556) | 1,234 | 0.249 kW |

The capped model forecast 0.393 kW; uncapped, 0.078 kW. #1556 is in 0.94.442
and 443, but the served model had been trained at 06:06 under 0.94.439 and the
upgrades did not retrain it. `nimbus_load.retrain` (household, 13:18 for the
laundry, then all 25 models) took it to 3,557 points and a 0.056 kW mean;
AC B1 went from 505 to 3,297 points, the pools from ~1,070 to ~3,450.
**Lesson:** a training fix reaches a served model only at its next retrain;
force one after an upgrade that changes training.
