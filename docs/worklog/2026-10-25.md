synthetically fake the spike and see how it did on devhub", and "It has been
decided u knew that": the 18 Oct decision was a synthetic test, and it took
until today to act on it.

On devhub, with two synthetic price sensors carrying $15/kWh in one
half-hour slot as the third source, and consolidation switched on for the
test only (the household agreed at 18:52; production's switch read off
before, during and after, through the REST API):

- Consolidation kept the spike only one source saw: $15.00 in the slot.
- The plan acted on it: 40 kW export in the slot (11.5 kW with consolidation
  off), SoC 58.2% entering it (48.8%), holding charge in the run-up.
- Live dispatch kept the primary's price for the current interval, by design
  ("the settled block is never touched"), so it did not sell at the
  secondary's $15. A real spike moves the primary's own price.
- Restored: synthetic sensors removed, source 3 back on the tariff sensors,
  consolidation off.

## #2054 (#2045): a hang, found and fixed

#2054's Unit Tests ran to the 60-minute limit. Diagnosed on CI (a temporary
`diag/2054-hang` workflow, since deleted), not on the laptop:

- `test_offer_curve.py::test_a_shared_chargers_curve_points_are_what_a_fresh_solve_does[paid_all_day]`
  passes on `main` in 3 s and hung on the branch.
- The 52nd re-solve was handed a NaN cost; HiGHS's dual simplex never returns
  on one, whatever its iteration and time limits (HiGHS log: `Objective -nan`).
- The NaN came from the offer curve's retail probe: a retail import of exactly
  -1.00, the domain floor, with an unbounded ranging end, made the probe -inf,
  and `plateau_end` computed -inf - -inf. Latent on `main`; #2045's larger
  penalty made HiGHS report the unbounded end.
- Fix (a27423a2): each end is probed only inside the domain and on its own
  side of the price; `sweep_costs_with_ranging` refuses a non-finite cost.
  Both new tests were run against `main`'s solver on CI and failed there.
