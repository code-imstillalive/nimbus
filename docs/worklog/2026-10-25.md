## #2054 (#2045): a hang and a wrong offer-curve point, found and fixed

#2054's Unit Tests ran to the 60-minute limit. Diagnosed on CI (temporary
`diag/*` workflows, all since deleted), not on the laptop:

- `test_offer_curve.py::test_a_shared_chargers_curve_points_are_what_a_fresh_solve_does[paid_all_day]`
  passes on `main` in 3 s and hung on the branch.
- **The hang:** the 52nd re-solve was handed a NaN cost; HiGHS's dual simplex
  never returns on one, whatever its iteration and time limits (HiGHS log:
  `Objective -nan`). The NaN came from the offer curve's retail probe: a retail
  import of exactly -1.00, the domain floor, with an unbounded ranging end, made
  the probe -inf, and `plateau_end` computed -inf - -inf. Fix (a27423a2): each
  end is probed only inside the domain and on its own side of the price;
  `sweep_costs_with_ranging` refuses a non-finite cost. Both new tests were run
  against `main`'s solver on CI and failed there.
- **Then a wrong point:** at -0.54 the curve read 3 kW where a fresh solve
  plans 0. Ruled out on CI: the costs (identical to a fresh solve's, coefficient
  for coefficient) and the exact budget (unlimited fails the same way). Cause:
  the plan's own direction binaries are fixed at the retail answer in the base
  solve, so a one-way point at another price is optimal only within that
  pattern. Fix (7424220c): such a point is checked by the exact MIP, within the
  existing budget.
- **Golden master** (6b65a80a): `rich_site_native_with_ev`'s import curve now
  reads 19.262 kW at -1.00 $/kWh, where the old snapshot read 0 kW at every
  price. Regenerated on CI; the other 21 scenarios are unchanged.

Both bugs are pre-existing on `main`; the correct penalty made the plan's
own direction round engage on that site and exposed them.
