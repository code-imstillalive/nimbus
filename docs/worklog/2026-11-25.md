## Tester's 32 kW charge (open)

- A tester saw a ~32 kW charge late morning. Two guesses were ruled out by their data: not a re-plan flip-flop (the plan never asked for it), and not mode-before-setpoint ordering (the inverter limit held 25-29 kW for 13 minutes). The plan asked for a deliberate 12-21 kW grid charge at ~11c while the blueprint sent 25-29 kW; their set-power action passes `power_kw` through unchanged and they have only the home battery. The logbook then showed only the dispatch wrote the limit, varying second by second: the allowed-charge (site import) cap trimming a larger request. The dispatch's mode record alternated CHG / HOLD / SC, so it followed the plan (no manual mode). Most likely the price-change rule (`now_i`, 22 Nov): on a price change the blueprint follows the current minute's row, which the Battery Forecast state does not show (their 13:16 diagnostics: state -7.7 kW, the 13:20 row -18.4 kW at a price step).
