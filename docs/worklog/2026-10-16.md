## The day's two failures

1. **AEMO false spike.** `CA_BRIS_59E3F2B6_1` was applied in pre-dispatch at about half dispatch's limit, giving $9-23/kWh for 13:00-16:00. P5MIN violated it by 560-980 MW at ~9c from 09:55, and the spike was gone by the 10:55 P5MIN run.
   - **Why rule (a) missed it:** it needs a P5MIN spike, and there wasn't one.
   - **Rule (c)'s evidence:** RHS/(RHS+violation) ≤ 0.8 on the first interval's `CA_` violation. On the fixtures it was 0.52-0.75 on 15 Oct and 0.55-0.70 on 16 Oct; the 11:10 run's 79 MW violation at normal prices read 0.96. No 9 Oct run (a real spike) violates any `CA_`.
   - **Replay:** on the day's NEMWEB files, rule (c) arms at 09:50 and releases at 10:55.
2. **LocalVolts stall.** The rows' `lastUpdate` stayed at 00:01:55Z from 10:01 to 13:00 (peak age 175 min, Mark's measurement) while polling continued.
   - **Effect:** at 12:30 the reference household sold 36-39 kW into the stale $1.01/kWh.
   - **Fix (#1793):** past 60 min (measured normal max 28.4 min over 30 h), the plan prices from AEMO plus the learned markup, and the oldest stamp decides. LV v2 2.11.0's `source_updated` could read a stall as fresh (Mark), so `current_buy_rate`'s `lastUpdate` is always cross-checked.
   - **Live check:** on devhub at 12:45 the fallback logged a 163-min stale source.
   - **Upstream:** raised as purcell-lab/localvolts_v2#52.

**Two-stage stochastic (#1805), devhub timeout fixed.**
- **The failure:** devhub's joint MIP (4,128 binaries per scenario copy, from the test controllable loads) stopped at its 20 s limit with an incumbent at `mip_gap` 3.7e-5. The pinned-LP refresh after it then failed in 0.0 s.
- **The cause:** HiGHS's `time_limit` counts every `run()` on one `Highs` object. Reproduced in isolation; a limit of `getRunTime() + 5` makes the refresh solve.
- **The fix:** `LPProblem.accept_time_limited_mip_gap`, which only the joint solve sets (1e-3). Every other caller is unchanged.
- **Production's live solve, replayed offline on the branch:** stochastic `optimal` in 2.75 s, 12,249 variables, non-anticipativity gap 0.0.
- **Calibrated bands:** the scenarios now use #1807's calibrated p10/p90 when present, else the envelope. The summary says which, in `solar_band`.

**Per-battery SoC on the Solver tab (#1810).**
- **The problem:** on Chris's site (home battery plus an EV participant) the Plan chart drew the fleet blend. Home was at 92.99% and the EV at 73.0%, so it showed ~86%, which no meter reads.
- **The fix:** one SoC sensor per battery, created only on multi-battery sites, and one chart line each. Production resolves to its unchanged chart. Validated on devhub's two-battery set-up ("Test EV").
- **Found while building it:** Charge By binds the home battery, but its published shortfall read the fleet SoC. Fixed in the same PR.
