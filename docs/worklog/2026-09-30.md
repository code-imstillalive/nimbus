## The findings worth carrying

- **#1463 / #1477 (Mark's install).** A scorecard `unavailable` for 6+ hours had two
  layers: the publisher pushed nothing when the new day could not be scored, so the push
  sensor's 5-minute staleness rule blanked it; and the day could not be scored because
  **HA's recorder writes a row only on a change** -- PV holding 0.0 from 17:39 read as a
  6-hour coverage gap. Held values now count to the window end unless the raw history
  shows an `unavailable`/`unknown` transition.
- **#1480 -- the "~$2/day path delta" is two LP-only tie-breakers.** Reproduced the
  reference household's scoring locally, read-only, matching its published figures to the
  cent; a term-by-term decomposition of the oracle objective put the whole $1.84 in the
  #731 round-trip-loss term ($1.61) and the #692 charge-earliness term (~$0.21). A first
  hypothesis (the export bonus) was posted and then **retracted on measurement**: LP bonus
  revenue and the evaluator's credit were identical to the cent. Regret and EPR were always
  right; `j_star_path_delta_explained`/`_unexplained` now say so. It also closes the
  "at least one more term" in #1081's pinned xfail.
- **#1360.** Production's 3,629 healthy cycles: calibration fallback on **0**; but
  `simplex_iterations` median **0**, so the decision table's "healthy = tens of thousands"
  (devhub's number) cannot discriminate there.
- **#1396.** Two restarts logged zero managed skips, yet devhub's managed solver entities
  still carry production's data -- the rule stands: nothing read from them is devhub's.
