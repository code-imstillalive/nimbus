### The Regret tab stalled a small install (#2276; 0.94.519)

A tester replaced their tabs through the Tabs card. While they deleted and renamed the old ones, their Home Assistant stopped responding: a five-minute gap in all their data, with the CPU running hot. It came back when they closed the Nimbus browser tabs. Their diagnostics (read 19:10) showed a healthy install afterwards: 0.94.518, plan `optimal` in 0.3 s, no Repairs.

- **Cause, measured on the test box:** the Regret tab's three cards each call `compute_quality_report` for their day on every render (a page load, and every save in the dashboard editor), a full hindsight MILP each. One day took 4.8 s; three at once 15 s. Not seen in their log, which the diagnostics don't carry; inferred.
- **Fix:** one report at a time, and a window scored in the last 10 minutes answered from memory; `rescore_history` clears it. Fail-once on CI: run 41997308014, three solves for one day; a first attempt compared times as strings and failed for that reason, so it was redone before counting it.
