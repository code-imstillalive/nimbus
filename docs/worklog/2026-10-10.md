## P5MIN into the solve (#1692): built, then parked on evidence

The household asked for Nimbus to see a forecast spike and precharge on its
own arithmetic, with no special rule. #1692 priced the next hour from P5MIN.
Two findings along the way stand on their own:

- **Wholesale -> retail is linear, and the existing learner pairs the wrong
  intervals.** Measured from production's recorder: LocalVolts export is
  1.0605 x RRP to 0.05 c, import 1.1666 x RRP + network. LocalVolts posts a
  provisional price ~3 s into an interval and the real one ~15-50 s in;
  AEMO's current-price sensor posts ~78 s in. `compute_5min_offset()`'s
  "at or before" pairing therefore matches each retail price with the
  PREVIOUS interval's wholesale. #1692's `fit_markup()` (settled value per
  interval, paired on the interval, slope from within-half-hour-same-day
  movement, recent-median intercepts) learned 1.0595x / 1.1656x on devhub
  from devhub's own data. **Filed as #1694** for the tail, not fixed there.
- **On 9 Oct, following the latest P5MIN run made the morning worse, not
  better.** Runs swung between "no spike" (04:35, 04:41, 04:55) and
  $19,864-22,563/MWh (04:50, 05:00) while the real peak was $860. With the
  block in progress priced at its settled live price, the overlay sold 40 kW
  at 04:40 and lost ~$1.20 against no overlay.

The household and Mark ("don't accept AEMO forecast") parked it: #1692 is a
draft. **Two replays posted on it earlier were invalid and are retracted on
the PR**: the script priced the block in progress at predispatch (~$0.25
during a $0.83 spike), which flips the sign of anything near a spike.

## A windowed soft reserve (#1695), and a 9 Oct replay on the forecasts Nimbus actually read

#1667's soft reserve, plus **Battery Reserve From / Until**. Inside the
window the bottom X% is drawn only when the price beats its later value plus
the release price; outside it there is no reserve, so it can be sold as the
window closes. From == Until keeps the all-day reserve.

**The release price is a margin on top of the energy's later value, not a
"sell above X" threshold.** I explained it to the household as a threshold
first; the PR's own tests caught it. A margin above the overnight import
price makes the house import rather than use the reserve. #1667's CHANGELOG
wording is corrected in #1695.

Mark asked for a fair comparison. Production's recorder kept LocalVolts'
`forecast` attribute at every update on 9 Oct, so the replay priced each step
from the LocalVolts forecast as it stood, with the block in progress at
LocalVolts' recorded live price:

| 9 Oct 04:20-06:00 | net grid cashflow | end SoC |
|---|---:|---:|
| no reserve | $5.74 | 2.0% |
| 15%, 00:00-05:30, release 0.10 | $7.94 | 2.0% |
| 15%, 00:00-05:30, release 0.30 | $7.94 | 2.0% |

Limits are on #1695: the battery was already below 15% at 04:20, the overnight
holding cost is outside the window, and it is one event. Sizing should come
from EPR regret (Mark).
