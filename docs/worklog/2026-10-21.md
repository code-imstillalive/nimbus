## Findings

- **#1964: the export bonus scattered ON/OFF/ON again.** Its 1e-7 latest-first
  tie-break lost to the solver's tolerance when the cap left a partial period.
  Found by the v2 property on CI; fixed in #1966 (secondary channel).
- **#1963: since 0.94.206 an adequacy load's value credit applied only to
  windowed loads.** Found by the #1837 cutover; neither test set had caught it.
- **#1981: a load signal's shut period could read `degenerate: false`.** The
  flag followed HiGHS's unclamped ranging, so another load in the same solve
  could turn it off. Fix in #1982 (awaiting review).
- **#1985: a battery's ranged headroom could exceed its physical headroom.**
  HiGHS's bound ranging for a variable at its upper bound is not capped there:
  a battery charging flat out read 0.32 kW of room it did not have. Fix in
  #1986 (awaiting review).
- **#1987: a battery above its Max SoC with terminal-value tiers made the whole
  solve infeasible** when it could not get back under by the tier period. #328
  had folded underfill into the tier row; the ceiling side was never done. Fix
  in #1988 (awaiting review); three golden snapshots refreshed, solver noise only.
- **#481 with #897: past about 0.1/h a leakier tank plans less heat.** The coast
  reference front-loads decay and the idle-fraction scaling cancels it in the
  full-power periods. Monotone below 0.1/h at every ambient scanned; a hot-water
  tank sits near 0.01-0.03/h. Recorded in the cutover ledger, not changed.
