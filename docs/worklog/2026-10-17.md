## #1825: stale prices in the score

- **What happened on 16 Oct:** LocalVolts stopped rebuilding its rows at 10:01. The rows carried a withdrawn pre-dispatch spike until 13:01. The Solver already refused to plan on it (#1793). But the next morning's quality report scored the day against it: EPR 43.4%, $28.93 regret.
- **The trap:** the recorder stores a row only when a state changes. So a refresh stamp's age has to be read **when its row was recorded**, not at the period being scored. Read at the period, a buy rate holding 0.0 all evening looked stale from 17:35 to 20:15 and from 21:05 to 23:55. Read at record time, 16 Oct is stale 11:10-13:20, and 13-15 Oct never pass 57 minutes.
- **The fix:** stale periods are re-priced from AEMO's recorded price plus the learned markup. This uses the Solver's own rule (stamp over an hour old) and applies to J_ref, J_ach and J_star alike.
- **Read-only replay of 16 Oct against production's recorder:**
  - on v0.94.461 code: 41.23% EPR, $28.93 regret (the published regret, to the cent);
  - on v0.94.462: **88.39%, $3.47**.
- **Report schema 4,** so the latest published day re-scores itself on upgrade.
