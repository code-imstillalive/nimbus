# Worklog 28 Nov 2026

All of today's work is on PR #2646, the solar forecaster's days 3-5 plus the Solar Lab tab, stacked on #2645 and #2644. Nothing merged to `main` today. All three PRs are green and waiting for Mark's full review.

## Days 3-5 and the plan

- All three models (ECMWF, GFS, GEFS) now go to 120 h, with a learned bias. Days 3-5 are planned on only once they have been learned, and the night is never downloaded for solar.
- `plan_until`: the plan only takes the days the forecaster has proved itself on.
- The first forecast comes once a week of history is in, rather than after the whole history.
- A model saved without a current tier is retrained at start, so days 3-5 appear straight after the upgrade.
- Weather decode and training run in a low-priority worker process, never inside Home Assistant.
  - Two fixes came from this. The worker now gets the config folder on its path, because it could not import `custom_components`. And `BrokenProcessPool` is imported directly, because the no-worker path crashed instead of stopping cleanly. Its test caught that.

## Solar Lab tab and card

- The lab dashboard's layout is now inside Nimbus. Labels never collide or get cut off, and it fits a phone.
- Before the first forecast, the card shows how far the weather history has got ("N of 7 days").
- Other installed solar forecasts are found automatically and compared. Nimbus notes each one's "tomorrow" figure at 21:00 itself, so the scoreboard can judge it even where the recorder does not keep that sensor.
- **Weather strip** under the chart, for day and night: sun, cloud, sprinkle, rain, heavy rain, showers with sun, and storm, each with a night version.
  - It comes from ECMWF `tp`, `cp` and `mucape`, plus the newest run's night steps (weather fields only).
  - It is animated simply (sun glow, rain flicker, lightning flash). It pauses when hidden and respects reduced motion.
  - It is built from the download alone, so a new install shows it within minutes.

## Weather entity and energy sensors

- New `weather.nimbus_weather`: now, hourly (3-hourly) and daily forecasts, with `rain_rate`, `shower_rate` and `storm_energy`.
- New `sensor.nimbus_solar_forecast_today`, `_today_remaining` and `_tomorrow`: kWh p50, with p10 and p90.
- **Fresher weather:** ECMWF's 12Z run is used for the weather only, for the next 48 h. It adds about 40 MB a day; the total is about 230 MB a day in summer.
  - The 12Z run is laid over the 00Z run, including columns the older file lacks.
  - Measured first: over 45 days, no newer run forecast a day's solar better than the evening-before 00Z. Daily error was 14.4 % for 00Z, 14.4 % for 12Z, 15.1 % for 06Z and 15.3 % for 18Z. So the solar forecast is unchanged.

## Energy dashboard

- `energy.py` gives Home Assistant's Energy dashboard the forecast as Wh per hour. Today's earlier hours are saved, so they survive a restart.
- Home Assistant's Energy settings list only `integration_type: service` entries as solar forecasts. Nimbus is a hub, so it never appears there.
  - Instead, a new option (*Show it in the Energy dashboard for*) names the Energy solar source.
  - Nimbus adds itself to that source and puts itself back when the Energy settings are saved without it.
  - A Repair appears if the source is deleted.
- Mark noted the listener cleanup reaches into the Energy manager's private `_update_listeners`. A comment there now says this is deliberate. The cleanup also skips quietly if a future Home Assistant reshapes that list; before, it would have raised on unload.
