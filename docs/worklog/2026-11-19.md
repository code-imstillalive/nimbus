## Solcast day 3-7 forecasts (#2560, PR #2561)

- **Found** from a tester's diagnostics (Solcast as the only solar source, auto-include on): the plan's solar was 100.2 kWh for tomorrow, matching Solcast, but 0.0 kWh on each of days 3, 4 and 5, midday included.

- **Fix:** the key list covers all seven days. The auto-include fetch, the device-contract member, the #546 dedup and the #1784 running-low gate all read that one list.
